🎓 Personal Introduction

I am currently a PhD student at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the supervision of Prof Yanbing Liu.

My research focuses on multimodal sarcasm detection and LLM security, exploring how AI systems can better understand and secure human communication across different modalities. I am passionate about bridging the gap between artificial intelligence and human communication, particularly in understanding nuanced expressions like sarcasm across multiple modalities.

🔬 Research Interests

🤖

Multimodal AI

Understanding cross-modal interactions in human communication, focusing on how different modalities (text, image, audio) work together to convey meaning and emotion.

🔤

Natural Language Processing

Processing and understanding human language at scale, with particular emphasis on sentiment analysis, sarcasm detection, and contextual understanding.

🛡️

LLM Security

Ensuring safety and robustness of large language models, developing frameworks to protect against adversarial attacks and ensure reliable AI systems.
